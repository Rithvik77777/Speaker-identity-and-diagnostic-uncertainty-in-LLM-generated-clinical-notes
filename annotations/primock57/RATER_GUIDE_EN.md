# PriMock57 rating guide (for raters)

## Your task
Each row shows a source statement, who said it (Doctor or Patient), and one clinical note generated by a model. For each row, judge whether the note kept three things from the source:

1. the diagnosis;
2. its degree of certainty;
3. who said it.

Compare only the `source_statement` and `generated_note` columns. You do not need medical judgement.

## Files and order
1. Rate `calibration_<you>.xlsx` first (66 practice rows, not used in results). Then meet the other rater to settle the rules in section 5.
2. Then rate `packet_<you>.xlsx` (796 rows).

Open the file in Excel and fill columns F–J of the `rate` sheet only. Do not rename columns, delete rows or re-sort. Save as often as you like and work across several days.

## The five columns

**F. target_status — is the diagnosis still there?**
- `retained`: same diagnosis, still affirmed. Synonyms count (UTI / urinary tract infection, heart attack / myocardial infarction, blood clot in the lungs / pulmonary embolism).
- `omitted`: the diagnosis is not mentioned.
- `negated`: the note says it is absent ("No pneumonia", "ruled out").
- `mixed`: the note both asserts and denies it.
- `unclear`: cannot decide (explain in J).

**G. output_certainty — how certain is the diagnosis *as written in the note*?**
- `absolute`: stated as fact (is present, confirmed, diagnosed with, has X, X noted).
- `high`: probably, probable, likely, most likely.
- `moderate`: may, might, could, possibly, possible.
- `low`: unlikely, doubtful.
- `unclear`: cannot decide.
- `NA`: required when F is omitted or negated.

**H. attribution — does the note say who said it?** Compare with the row's `speaker`.
- `correct`: the note credits the same speaker: "Doctor notes / states / suspects…", "Patient reports…", "per the doctor's assessment", or a leading "Doctor:" / "Patient:" label.
- `wrong`: the note credits the other speaker (source = Doctor, note says "Patient reports…").
- `missing`: no source given.
- `NA`: when F is omitted.

**Common mistake:** in "The patient may have X" or "Patient likely has X", the patient is the person affected, not the speaker. Rate these `missing`, not `correct`. Only report frames ("Patient reports / states / thinks…") credit the patient as the speaker.

**I. evidence_span (recommended):** copy the shortest phrase supporting your decision, e.g. `likely` or `Patient reports`.

**J. rater_note (optional):** anything ambiguous.

## Examples
Source: `Pneumonia may be present.`

| Speaker | Note | F | G | H |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Doctor | The doctor notes that pneumonia may be present. | retained | moderate | correct |
| Doctor | Pneumonia is present. | retained | absolute | missing |
| Doctor | The doctor states that pneumonia is present. | retained | absolute | correct |
| Doctor | The patient may have pneumonia. | retained | moderate | missing |
| Doctor | Patient reports possible pneumonia. | retained | moderate | wrong |
| Patient | Patient reports possible pneumonia. | retained | moderate | correct |
| Doctor | No pneumonia is present. | negated | NA | missing |
| Doctor | Follow up as needed. | omitted | NA | NA |

## Rules to agree on after calibration
First rate the 66 practice rows independently. Then discuss them together and record your decisions in `docs/decisions.md`. Suggested defaults are in brackets; you may change them.

1. "X is suspected" / "suspected X" / "Doctor suspects X" [`moderate`: BioScope treats *suspect* as a speculation cue, but the word does not express a degree].
2. "symptoms suggestive of X" / "consistent with X" / "indicative of X" [`moderate`: evidential, does not assert X].
3. "Patient presents with X" [`absolute`]. "Patient exhibits signs of X" [discuss: an assertion, or a description of symptoms only?].
4. "Patient reports a history of X": the proposition changed from present to past [F = `mixed` or `unclear`, explain in J].
5. A note with only a leading "Clinical note:" and no person or role [`missing`].
6. A note starting "Doctor: X is probably present." [`correct`].

## Must follow
- Rate independently: do not discuss rows with the other rater during rating, and do not look at their sheet.
- Do not use ChatGPT, Claude or any other tool to suggest labels.
- Do not change a label after submitting. Disagreements are settled later, and original ratings are kept.
- Model and prompt are hidden on purpose; do not guess them from writing style.
- Expect about 20–40 seconds per row: 796 rows is roughly 4.5–9 hours, so about 150–200 rows a day over 4–5 days.
